About Estara

Founder-led from strategy through delivery.

Estara is a London-based AI and software consultancy founded and led by James Gould, Managing Director. The senior technical lead remains directly involved in every engagement — from initial discovery through solution design, development and implementation — so decisions get made quickly and nothing is lost in handoffs.

Where a project genuinely requires additional expertise, trusted specialists are brought in for that work — with Estara retaining full responsibility and accountability for delivery from first call to final result.
